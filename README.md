# 🧪 The Alchemist’s Touch

A Fabric mod that **reinvents alchemy** in Minecraft.

New potions, effects, alchemical stones, imbued weapons, rituals, and immersive mechanics — **alchemy becomes strategic**, expanding far beyond simple brewing.

---

## 🧭 Overview

> The Alchemist is no longer just a potion brewer,  
> but a craftsman capable of **shaping matter, life, and death**.  
> Explore a complex potion system where every ingredient has meaning,  
> and your creations can change the world… literally.

### Main Features
- **30+ new potions** with unique gameplay effects.  
- **New status effects.**  
- **Alternative recipes** for some potions.  
- **Alchemical Stones**: apply effects to the **terrain**.  
- **Imbued Weapons**: apply a potion effect for a **number of hits based on the effect’s duration**.  
- **Potion-infused food**: consuming the food directly applies the effect.  
- **Stackable potions** up to 16 units, with an **internal cooldown**.
- **Rituals**: perform **blood-based transmutations** through the **Ritual Circle**.

---

### Early Game Potions

- <strong>Two new toxic crops</strong>: the Poisoned Carrot and Poisoned Beetroot, rare variants of their edible counterparts.  
- <strong>Lava buckets can now fuel brewing stands</strong>, though they provide much less fuel than blaze powder.  
- The <strong>Alchemist’s Core</strong>, a powerful craftable item, acts as a substitute for Nether Wart in brewing — though it’s much harder to obtain.

## 🔮 Rituals
Rituals are the **pinnacle of alchemical mastery** — the moment where blood, essence, and will converge to shape reality itself.  
Unlike potions, which merely channel existing energy, **rituals rewrite the flow of life**, granting permanent power… or invoking deadly consequences.

Each ritual requires:
- A **specific ingredient** placed upon the ritual circle.  
- A **clear set of conditions** (time, weather, health, ...).  
- A **blood cost** and **XP sacrifice** to fuel the transmutation.  

To begin a ritual, the alchemist must first **create the Ritual Circle** by using a **Blood Bag** on the ground.  
The blood within the bag seeps into the soil, forming the sacred symbol that binds life and death.  
Without this offering, no ritual can take shape — the circle simply cannot exist.

Success is never guaranteed: your **health**, **experience**, and **faith** determine the outcome.  
Perform them wisely — for failure can be as dangerous as the power you seek.

## ⚗️ Special Items

#### Alchemical Stones

<details>
<summary>Click to expand</summary>

| Acid | Destroys most weak blocks upon contact, mimicking acid corrosion. | Low-hardness blocks (dirt, wood, sand, etc.) | Acid Potion |

</details>

Items capable of **storing potion essences** and **applying the effect to the terrain** (e.g. acidification, petrification…).



#### Imbued Weapons

Weapons can be **charged with a potion effect**.  
Each effect lasts for a **number of hits based on the potion’s duration**, with a **minimum of 10 hits**.

#### Syringes

- Allow collecting blood by using them or hitting entities.  
- Can store an entity’s effects or inject them into another.
- 20 uses

#### Ingredients

- **Blood Bag**: stores collected blood (different blood types exist).  
- **Alchemist Core**: replaces Nether Wart in recipes, rarer.  
- **Witch’s Finger**, **Zombie Brain**, **Claw**, **Leaf**
